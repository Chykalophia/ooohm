# Date picker: design options

The popup that replaces the browser's native date picker on the request sentence (`DateToken`, `src/app/request-leave-sentence.tsx`). All three options meet the spec's R4, R5, R11 and R12: the typed field, then a header, a Monday-first 6-row grid and a "Today" footer. All three use only the app's theme tokens, copied from `src/app/globals.css`. They differ in the selected-day shape, the today marker, the header layout, the footer, and how the popup sits on a phone.

Each file has a working sentence at the top. Click a date, then try the arrow keys, PageUp/PageDown (Shift for a year), Home/End, Enter, Escape, typing `YYYY-MM-DD`, or clicking outside. Below it are fixed states: default with focus, hover, empty value, end date with a minimum (disabled days), a rejected typed entry, "Today" disabled, and a 320px phone. The bar at the top switches the theme (all 9), the mode (light, dark, automatic: Classic only), the corners and a custom accent. "Today" is pinned to Mon, Nov 9, 2026.

**Option A: Quiet popover** (`option-a.html`) ★ **my pick.** It is a sibling of the type and portion menus already in the sentence (`ChipSelect`): the same `rounded-xl` surface, border and shadow, opening right under the token. The month label is on the left and both arrows sit together on the right. The typed field is a compact field with an inline caption. The selected day is a filled accent chip shaped like the theme's buttons (`--button-radius`: a pill in Plum and Frost, square in Signal). Today is a small accent-ink dot under the number. "Today" is a quiet text action at the bottom right. On a phone it stays anchored under the token and slides left to keep a 1rem margin. *Trade-off:* it is the calmest and most consistent with the screen. But the dot is the least visible today marker, and the typed field is small.

**Option B: Framed form panel** (`option-b.html`). It reads like a small form. The typed field is full size with its label above, the month is centred between two bordered arrow buttons, and a hairline divides the weekday row. Cells are rounded squares that follow the theme's corner scale. Today is an accent-ink ring, and the selected day is a filled accent square. A full-width outline "Today" button sits under a hairline. On a phone it pins to the screen's side margins under the token's line. *Trade-off:* it has the clearest affordances: the ring is the strongest today marker, and the typed field is obvious. But it is the heaviest of the three, it has more borders than the "calm" rule likes, and its full-width "Today" button competes a little with picking a day.

**Option C: Sentence-scale calendar** (`option-c.html`). It carries the sentence's voice into the popup. A large month name is set in the display face (serif in Forest), with the year muted beside it. The arrows are bare accent-ink icons, the cells are generous, and the typed field is underline-only. Today is a short accent-ink underline under the number, and the selected day is a filled accent tile. The footer's "Today · Mon, Nov 9" shows the date it will pick. On a phone it becomes a panel the width of the card, with a smaller heading. *Trade-off:* it has the most character and fits the editorial sentence. But the big heading costs height on phones, and the underline-only field is the least conventional input.

## Note for Spec Revise

AC1(a) says November 2026's grid has "exactly the 7 dates outside November". The grid from 2026-10-26 to 2026-12-06 has **12**: Oct 26–31 (6) and Dec 1–6 (6). Nov 1, 2026 is a Sunday, so the Monday-first grid opens with six October days. The mockups' grid helper returns 12. AC1(a) should say 12.
