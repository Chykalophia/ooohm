Privacy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

This page covers ooohm.team, the website you're reading. OOOhm itself is software each company installs and runs on its own servers, and that is covered separately below.

Who we are

ooohm.team is run by Chykalophia Group, LLC, in the United States. Questions about this page or your data: support@chykalophia.com.

What this site collects

Almost nothing. The site has no sign-up, no forms, no analytics and no advertising trackers, and it loads nothing from other companies: fonts, pictures and videos are all served from ooohm.team.

The site is hosted by Vercel. Like any web host, Vercel's servers handle the technical details of each visit (your IP address, browser and the page you asked for) to deliver the page and keep it secure.

Cookies and browser storage

This site sets no cookies. If you pause the moving examples on the home page, your browser remembers that until you close the tab, using session storage. That choice never leaves your browser. Because nothing here needs your consent, there is no cookie banner.

Your OOOhm install

When a company runs OOOhm, the people, requests, balances and emails in it live on that company's own servers and database. Chykalophia Group, LLC does not host them, cannot see them and receives nothing from the app: it has no telemetry and does not report back to us. The company running the install decides how that data is handled, so questions about it go to your employer or whoever runs your OOOhm.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can ask what personal data we hold about you, have it corrected or deleted, or object to how it's used. Email support@chykalophia.com and we'll answer within 30 days. If you're in the EU or UK, you can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Changes

If this site starts collecting anything, for example cookieless visit counts, this page will say so first, and the date at the top will change.
